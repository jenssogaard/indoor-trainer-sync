How it works

1

Connect Suunto

Sign in once with your Suunto account. We never see your password.

2

Ride in Zwift

Zwift saves a standard FIT file for each ride on your Mac. The app picks up new rides automatically.

3

See it in Suunto

Each ride is uploaded to your Suunto account with power, heart rate and cadence intact.

Features

Automatic sync

New rides upload in the background. No exporting, no dragging files around.

Status and retry

A simple list shows every ride and its sync status, with one-click retry if an upload fails.

Original data

Your ride file is passed through unchanged, so Suunto calculates training load from real power data.

No duplicates

Each ride is uploaded once. Skip rides you also recorded on your watch.

No Zwift login

The app only reads ride files already on your computer. Your Zwift account is never touched.

Lightweight

A small native Mac app that stays out of your way.

FAQ

Which Zwift setups are supported?

Zwift running on a Mac. Zwift on Apple TV, iPad, iPhone or Android doesn't store ride files where other apps can reach them, so those aren't supported.

What shows up in Suunto?

Your ride appears in the Suunto app with its power, heart rate, cadence and speed, and counts toward training load (TSS, fitness, fatigue and form). Metrics that Suunto calculates only on the watch during a workout, such as recovery time, aren't available for uploaded workouts.

Do you need my Zwift or Suunto password?

No. Suunto uses a standard secure sign-in (OAuth), and the app never asks for your Zwift account.

What does it cost?

It's free.
