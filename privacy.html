Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how Sogaard Inc ("we", "us") handles information when you use ZwiftToSuunto (the "App"), a macOS app that uploads your Zwift ride files to your Suunto account.

What we collect

  • Suunto connection details. When you connect your Suunto account, Suunto gives us access tokens and your Suunto user ID. We use them only to upload workouts you've chosen to sync. We never receive your Suunto password.
  • Workout files. Ride files (FIT files) created by Zwift on your Mac pass through our service on their way to Suunto. They can contain time, duration, power, heart rate, cadence, speed and distance.
  • Sync history. The App keeps a list of rides and their sync status on your Mac.

We don't collect your Zwift credentials, and the App doesn't access your Zwift account.

How we use it

We use this information only to upload your rides to Suunto, show sync status and fix problems with the service.

No marketing, no third-party access

  • We never use your data for marketing, advertising or profiling, and we never send you marketing email.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and no third party gets access to it, except Suunto (where you asked us to send your rides) and the hosting provider listed below, which stores data only on our behalf.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking in the App or on this website.

How long we keep it

  • Workout files are deleted from our service once the upload to Suunto succeeds, and after 7 days at most if it keeps failing.
  • Suunto tokens are kept while your account is connected, and deleted when you disconnect.
  • Sync history stays on your Mac until you delete the App or clear it.

Service providers

We use Linode (Akamai Cloud) to run our service. It processes data only on our instructions and may not use it for its own purposes. Your workouts are sent to Suunto Oy, whose own privacy policy applies once they arrive in your Suunto account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and tokens are encrypted at rest.

Your rights

You can disconnect your Suunto account in the App at any time, which deletes your tokens from our service. You can also ask us to access, correct or delete any data we hold about you by emailing jens@jenssogaard.com. If you're in the EU, EEA or UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The App isn't intended for children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the App.
